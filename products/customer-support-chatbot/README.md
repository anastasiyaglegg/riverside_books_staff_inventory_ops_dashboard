# Product C — Customer Support Chatbot

Supporting product area for Riverside Books.

## Purpose

Help customers find dependable answers about books, store policies, and the buying journey, with human support available when the chatbot cannot help.

## First candidate intents

- Book discovery and recommendation clarification.
- Questions about availability or how to find a title.
- Store policies and common buying questions.
- Help locating the right human support path.

## Product requirements to validate

- Approved content sources and update ownership.
- Answer confidence, uncertainty language, and escalation rules.
- The minimum customer context needed for useful support.
- Conversation logging, privacy, and quality review expectations.

## Definition of a useful first slice

A customer can ask a small set of common questions, receive an answer grounded in approved Riverside Books content, and reach a human when the answer is uncertain or outside scope.
